    06 · Under the hood

    How good is the model?

    Measured on matches the model never saw during training, against a simple baseline: each player's average over their last five games.

    1. 1

      Scrape

      Every morning: fixtures, player histories, prices and injury news from the official FPL API, plus three past seasons for training.

    2. 2

      Engineer

      Form, minutes and start rate, expected goals and assists, fixture difficulty, price, and how strong both teams have been lately. Only past matches count.

    3. 3

      Train

      A gradient-boosted model is retrained on about 90,000 player-matches, then scaled by each player's chance of playing.

    4. 4

      Publish

      Predictions are checked and published here automatically.
